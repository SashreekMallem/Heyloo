## Who this covers

Heyloo provides an AI phone receptionist to businesses. This policy covers three groups of people:

- **Customers:** the business owners and staff who have a Heyloo account. For account and billing
  data, Heyloo is the controller: we decide how it is used.
- **Callers:** the people who phone or message a business that uses Heyloo. For callers' data, the
  business decides why it is collected, and Heyloo processes it on the business's behalf as its
  processor (or "service provider") under the [Data Processing Addendum](/legal/dpa).
- **Business contacts:** people at businesses we contact about Heyloo, and visitors to our website.

## If you called a business that uses Heyloo

The call you made was answered by an AI assistant working for that business. You were told at the
start of the call that you were speaking with an AI and that the call may be recorded. The
business uses Heyloo to answer your questions, book appointments or take orders, take messages and
pass them to its staff. The business controls this data: to access, correct or delete it, contact
the business you called. We will help them with your request. You can also write to
{{privacy_email}}, and we will pass your request to the business.

## What we collect

- **Account data:** name, email address, business name and type, phone numbers, business hours,
  services, prices and the other configuration you give us.
- **Call and message data:** the caller's phone number, call recordings, transcripts, AI-generated
  summaries, and the bookings, orders, messages and answers to intake questions the agent takes on
  the business's behalf.
- **Billing data:** plan, usage and invoices. Card details are collected and stored by our payment
  provider, never by Heyloo.
- **Business contact data:** business names, public phone numbers, websites, public reviews and work
  contact details from public sources and business-data providers, used to contact businesses that
  may want Heyloo.
- **Usage and device data:** pages visited, product usage, browser and device information, and error
  reports, used to keep the service running, secure and improving.

## How we use it

- To provide the service: answer and route calls, book appointments, take orders and messages,
  send owners alerts and summaries, and show everything in the dashboard.
- To bill for the service, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep accounts secure.
- To support customers, send service and account emails, and tell customers about changes.
- To contact businesses about Heyloo. Every marketing email includes a way to opt out.
- To monitor, debug and improve Heyloo, and to comply with the law.

We do not use callers' conversations to train our own AI models, and we do not use callers' data
for our own marketing.

## AI and recording disclosure

Callers are told at the start of every call that they are speaking with an AI and that the call
may be recorded. This is built into every agent and cannot be turned off.

## How long we keep it

- **Call recordings** are kept for the retention period set on the business's account, which is
  30 days unless the business changes it, and are then permanently deleted.
- **Transcripts, summaries, bookings and messages** are kept while the business's account is open,
  so it can review its call history, and are deleted on request, including when the account closes.
- **Account data** is kept while the account is open and is deleted on request once the account
  closes, except where we must keep records.
- **Billing records** are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

A customer can ask us to delete a specific recording or their whole account at any time, and we will
do so unless the law requires us to keep a record.

## Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We
share it only with the service providers needed to run Heyloo, each bound by a data processing
agreement. Depending on the features a business uses, they are:

- **Voice and telephony:** Retell AI (call handling), and Twilio or Telnyx (phone numbers and text
  messages).
- **Language models:** OpenAI (the voice agent's language model, through Retell), and Google (Gemini)
  and Anthropic (text-message conversations and writing features).
- **Infrastructure:** Supabase (database, authentication and file storage) and Vercel (website and
  app hosting).
- **Payments:** Stripe (subscriptions and card payments) and PayPal (referral partner payouts).
- **Email:** Microsoft 365 (sending account and alert emails).
- **Monitoring and analytics:** Sentry (error reports) and PostHog (product analytics).
- **Business-data providers:** providers of public business information, used only for business
  contact data.

We also share data when a business connects Heyloo to another service it chooses (such as its
booking software), when the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or as part of
a merger or sale of our business, in which case this policy continues to apply to it.

We will update this list before we add a provider that processes callers' data.

## Where data is stored

Heyloo's database is hosted in the United States. If you use Heyloo from outside the United States,
your data is transferred to and processed in the United States.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal data we hold about you,
the right to access, correct, export or delete it, to object to or restrict how it is used, to withdraw
consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Residents of California and other
US states with privacy laws have these rights under those laws, and we will not treat you
differently for using them.

To use your rights, email {{privacy_email}}. We will confirm your identity before acting on a
request and reply within the time the law requires. You may use an authorised agent. Callers should
contact the business they called first, as described above.

## Security

Every customer's data is isolated from every other customer's at the database level, access is
limited to people who need it, Heyloo's own admin access requires two-factor authentication, and data
is encrypted in transit and at rest. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we
will tell you as the law requires.

## Children

Heyloo is a service for businesses and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly
collect personal data from children through our website or accounts. If you believe we have, contact
{{privacy_email}} and we will delete it.
