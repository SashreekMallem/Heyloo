## What we collect

Call recordings and transcripts, business configuration you provide, and usage data needed to
operate and bill your account.

## AI and recording disclosure

Callers are told at the start of every call that they are speaking with an AI and that the call
may be recorded — this is compiled into every agent's greeting and cannot be turned off.

## Recording retention

Call recordings are retained for up to 90 days by default (pending final confirmation from
counsel), after which they are permanently deleted.

## Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the service providers necessary to operate
Heyloo (voice, telephony, payments, and messaging providers), each bound by a data processing
agreement.
