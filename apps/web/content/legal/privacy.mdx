## Who this covers

Heyloo provides an AI phone receptionist to businesses. This policy describes the personal data
Heyloo handles, for two groups of people: the business owners and staff who have a Heyloo account
("customers"), and the people who phone or message a customer's business ("callers"). For callers'
data, the business that uses Heyloo decides why it is collected and Heyloo acts as its processor
under the [Data Processing Addendum](/legal/dpa). For account and billing data, Heyloo is the
controller.

## What we collect

- **Account data:** name, email address, business name and type, phone numbers, business hours,
  services and the other configuration you give us.
- **Call and message data:** the caller's phone number, call recordings, transcripts, summaries,
  bookings and messages the agent takes on your behalf.
- **Billing data:** plan, usage and invoices. Card details are collected and stored by our payment
  provider, never by Heyloo.
- **Usage and diagnostics:** product analytics and error reports needed to keep the service
  running and to fix problems.

## AI and recording disclosure

Callers are told at the start of every call that they are speaking with an AI and that the call
may be recorded. This is compiled into every agent's greeting and cannot be turned off.

## Recording and transcript retention

Call recordings and transcripts are kept for the retention period set on your account, which is
30 days unless you change it in your settings, and are then permanently deleted. You can ask us to
delete a specific recording or your whole account at any time; we will do so unless the law
requires us to keep a record.

## Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with the service providers needed to run Heyloo,
each bound by a data processing agreement. Depending on the features you use, they are:

- **Voice and telephony:** Retell AI (call handling), Telnyx and Twilio (phone numbers and SMS).
- **Language models:** Google (Gemini) and Anthropic, used to understand and answer callers.
- **Infrastructure:** Supabase (database, authentication and file storage) and Vercel (hosting).
- **Payments:** Stripe (subscriptions and card payments) and PayPal (partner payouts).
- **Email:** Resend, or the mailbox you connect yourself.
- **Monitoring and analytics:** Sentry (error reports) and PostHog (product analytics).

We will update this list before we add a provider that processes caller data.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your
personal data, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to complain to your local data
protection authority. Customers can export or delete data from the dashboard or by contacting
support. If you are a caller and want your data removed, contact the business you phoned; we will
help them fulfil your request.

## Security

Every customer's data is isolated from every other customer's at the database level, access is
limited to people who need it, and data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the support option in your Heyloo dashboard or reply to any
email we send you.
