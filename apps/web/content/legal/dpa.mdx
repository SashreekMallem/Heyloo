This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) between
{{legal_name}}, {{address}} ("Heyloo"), and the business that uses Heyloo ("you"). It takes effect
on {{effective_date}} and applies whenever Heyloo processes personal data on your behalf. If this
DPA and the Terms conflict on data protection, this DPA wins.

## 1. Roles

For personal data about your callers and customers that Heyloo processes to provide the service,
you are the controller (or "business") and Heyloo is your processor (or "service provider"). Each
party will comply with the data protection laws that apply to it.

## 2. What we process

- **Purpose:** answering and routing your calls and messages, booking appointments, taking orders
  and messages, sending you alerts and summaries, and supporting and securing the service.
- **People:** your callers and customers, and your staff who use Heyloo.
- **Data:** phone numbers, names, call recordings, transcripts, AI-generated summaries, messages,
  bookings, orders and answers to your intake questions, plus any other information callers choose
  to share.
- **Duration:** for as long as you use Heyloo, then until deleted under section 8.

You must not configure Heyloo to collect special categories of data, payment card numbers or
government ID numbers, and must not use it for protected health information unless we have signed
a Business Associate Agreement.

## 3. Our commitments

Heyloo will:

- process personal data only on your documented instructions, which are these Terms, this DPA and
  your configuration and use of the service, and tell you if we believe an instruction breaks the
  law;
- not sell or share the data, not use it for any purpose other than providing the service to you,
  and not combine it with data from other sources except as the law permits a service provider to;
- make sure everyone authorised to process the data is bound by confidentiality; and
- tell you if we can no longer meet our obligations under the laws that apply.

## 4. Security

Heyloo applies appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect the data, including:
isolating each customer's data from every other customer's at the database level; encryption in
transit and at rest; access limited to people who need it, with two-factor authentication for
Heyloo's own admin access; signature verification on incoming provider webhooks; logging of
administrative actions; and automatic deletion of call recordings at the end of your retention
period.

## 5. Sub-processors

You authorise Heyloo to use the sub-processors listed in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).
Each is bound by a written agreement with data protection terms no less protective than this DPA,
and Heyloo remains responsible for their performance. We will update that list before adding or
replacing a sub-processor that processes callers' data. You may object on reasonable data
protection grounds by emailing {{privacy_email}} within 30 days; if we cannot resolve the
objection, you may cancel the affected service.

## 6. Helping you

Taking into account the nature of the processing, Heyloo will help you respond to requests from
people exercising their data protection rights, and will provide information you reasonably need
for data protection impact assessments and consultations with regulators. If a caller contacts us
directly, we will pass the request to you and will not answer it ourselves unless you ask us to.

## 7. Personal data breaches

Heyloo will tell you without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting
your data, with the information we have about what happened, the data affected, and what we are
doing about it, and will update you as we learn more.

## 8. Deletion and return

Call recordings are deleted at the end of the retention period set on your account, which is
30 days unless you change it. Transcripts, summaries, bookings and messages are kept while your account
is open so you can review your call history, and Heyloo will delete any of them on your request. When your account ends, Heyloo will, at your request, return
a copy of your data and then delete it, except where the law requires us to keep it.

## 9. Audits

Heyloo will make available the information reasonably needed to show that it meets this DPA. If
that is not enough, you may, once a year and at your own cost, request an audit on 30 days' written
notice, carried out during business hours by an independent auditor bound by confidentiality, in a
way that does not disrupt the service or expose other customers' data.

## 10. International transfers

Heyloo processes data in the United States. Where the law requires a transfer mechanism for data
transferred to the United States, the parties agree to rely on the applicable Standard Contractual
Clauses, which are incorporated by reference.
