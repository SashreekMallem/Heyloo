## Scope

This Data Processing Addendum governs Heyloo's processing of personal data on your behalf as a
processor, including caller phone numbers, call recordings, and transcripts.

## Sub-processors

Heyloo relies on the voice, telephony, payments, and messaging sub-processors named in our
Privacy Policy. We will notify you of any material change to this list.

## Data retention

Call recordings are retained for up to 90 days by default (pending final confirmation from
counsel); you may request deletion at any time subject to legal record-keeping requirements.
