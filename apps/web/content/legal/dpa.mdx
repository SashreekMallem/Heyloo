## Scope

This Data Processing Addendum governs Heyloo's processing of personal data on your behalf as a
processor, including caller phone numbers, call recordings, transcripts and messages. You are the
controller of that data; Heyloo processes it only to provide the service you signed up for and on
your documented instructions (your configuration and use of the service).

## Confidentiality and security

Heyloo keeps caller data confidential, limits access to staff who need it, isolates each
customer's data from every other customer's, and applies appropriate technical and organisational
measures, including encryption in transit and at rest. We will tell you without undue delay if we
become aware of a personal data breach affecting your data.

## Sub-processors

Heyloo uses the voice, telephony, language-model, infrastructure, payments, email and monitoring
providers listed in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy). Each is bound by a written data
processing agreement no less protective than this one. We will update that list before adding a
provider that processes caller data, and you may object to a new sub-processor by contacting
support.

## Assisting you

We will help you respond to requests from callers to access, correct or delete their data, and
provide information reasonably needed to show compliance with your data protection obligations.

## Data retention and deletion

Call recordings and transcripts are kept for the retention period set on your account (30 days
unless you change it) and then permanently deleted. When your account ends we delete or return
your data on request, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.
