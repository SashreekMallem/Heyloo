## The service

Heyloo provides an AI phone receptionist for your business. By creating an account you confirm
that you are authorised to act for your business and that you accept these terms.

## AI and recording disclosure

Every call answered by a Heyloo AI agent opens with a disclosure that the caller is speaking
with an artificial intelligence assistant and that the call may be recorded. This disclosure is
compiled into every agent and cannot be disabled or edited by a tenant. You are responsible for
using the service in line with the recording and call-consent laws that apply to your business.

## Number port-out guarantee

If you choose to leave Heyloo, we guarantee a smooth port-out of your business phone number to
a new provider. Your number belongs to your business — Heyloo will never hold it hostage.

## Recording retention

Call recordings and transcripts are kept for the retention period set on your account, which is
30 days unless you change it in your settings, after which they are permanently deleted. See the
[Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) and the [Data Processing Addendum](/legal/dpa) for how we handle
caller data.

## Billing and cancellation

Plans are billed monthly or annually in advance. Usage past your plan's included minutes is
billed per minute at your plan's stated overage rate — there is no per-call penalty. You can
manage or cancel your plan from the billing portal in your dashboard; cancellation takes effect
at the end of the period you have already paid for.

## Acceptable use

You will not use Heyloo to break the law, to deceive callers about talking to an AI, to harass
people, or to send messages that people have not agreed to receive. We may suspend an account
that does, and will tell you why.

We may update these terms; we will tell account owners about material changes before they take
effect.
