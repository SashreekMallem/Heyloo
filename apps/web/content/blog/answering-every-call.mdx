---
title: "Why answering every call is the highest-leverage thing a small business can do"
excerpt: "Missed calls aren't a nuisance — for most service businesses, they're the single largest source of lost revenue."
date: "2026-08-01"
---

Every missed call is a customer who called your competitor next. For an auto shop, a vet
clinic, or a law firm, that single call can be worth thousands of dollars in lifetime value —
and most small businesses miss somewhere between a quarter and half of the calls that come in
during a normal week.

The fix isn't hiring another front-desk person for the hours nobody wants to work. It's making
sure the phone is always answered, by something that can actually book the appointment, not
just take a message.
