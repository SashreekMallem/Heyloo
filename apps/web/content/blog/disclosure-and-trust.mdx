title: "Why every Heyloo call starts with a disclosure — and why that's good for you"
excerpt: "Every Heyloo agent tells callers up front that they're talking to an AI, and that the call may be recorded. Here's why that's a feature, not friction."
date: "2026-08-15"
---

Some AI answering services try to sound as human as possible and hope nobody notices. We do
the opposite: every call opens with a short, clear disclosure that the caller is speaking with
an AI assistant, and that the call may be recorded.

That's not just a compliance checkbox. Callers who know what they're talking to ask clearer
questions, trust the booking they get at the end of the call, and are far less likely to feel
tricked later. Trust is the whole product here — we're not going to undercut it for a slightly
smoother-sounding greeting.
