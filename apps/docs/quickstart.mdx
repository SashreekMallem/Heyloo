---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Go live with your AI receptionist in under 5 minutes."
---

1. **Sign up** at [heyloo.com/signup](https://heyloo.com/signup) and choose your business type.
2. **Forward your number** — pick your carrier and follow the on-screen codes. Conditional
   forwarding is the default, so calls only route to Heyloo when you don't answer or your line
   is busy.
3. **Test it** — the setup wizard places a test call to confirm forwarding is working.
4. **Customize your agent** — set your hours, services, and any special instructions from
   Agent Settings in your dashboard.

That's it — calls to your number now reach your AI receptionist.
