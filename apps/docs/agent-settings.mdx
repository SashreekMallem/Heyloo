---
title: "Agent settings"
description: "Greeting, hours, services, FAQ, and AI instructions."
---

Your agent's settings live under Agent in your dashboard, split into a few tabs:

- **Greeting & Persona** — your AI's name. The disclosure sentence itself is compiled in and
  can't be edited.
- **Hours** — your weekly schedule plus one-off holiday exceptions.
- **Services** — the appointment types your AI can book, with duration and price.
- **FAQ** — question/answer pairs your AI can answer directly.
- **AI Instructions** — special instructions, your transfer number, voicemail message, and
  vertical-specific details.

Changes take effect within about 30 seconds of saving.
