Heyloo is an AI receptionist that answers your business phone 24/7, books real appointments
into your calendar, and delivers the details by SMS and email. Every call opens with a
disclosure that the caller is speaking with an AI and that the call may be recorded — this is
compiled into your agent and can't be turned off.
