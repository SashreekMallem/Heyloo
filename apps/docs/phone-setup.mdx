## Conditional vs. full forwarding

Conditional forwarding (the default) only sends a call to Heyloo when you don't answer or your
line is busy — it preserves your existing fallback if Heyloo is ever briefly unavailable. Full
forwarding sends every call immediately, which is faster to set up but has no fallback.

## Porting your number in

If you'd rather Heyloo own your number outright, request a port-in from the phone setup page.
This is a carrier-to-carrier process that typically takes several business days — you'll see a
status timeline rather than a simple pass/fail check.
